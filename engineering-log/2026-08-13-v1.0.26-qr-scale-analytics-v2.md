# 2026-08-13 - v1.0.26 QR Scale + Analytics v2

## Requirement

User explicitly requested the actual solution rather than more theory/documentation. My QR Codes is too bulky and Analytics is too small. Target is lakhs of QR codes and approximately 100 lakh / 10 million accesses while retaining Copy Source Link and Open/Show QR anytime.

## Preserved scope

No regression to Google Sheet parsing/sync, Today/Weekly timetable, Sheet Updates, Test Monitor, Content, Notifications, QR identity modes, verified-scan semantics, anti-bot behavior, templates, bulk generation, QR styling, lifecycle actions or shared-asset safety.

## Implementation

- New QR scale API with cursor-paged QR list, aggregate-backed analytics and cursor-paged recent scans.
- Compact My QR Codes table/card UI with Copy Source, Copy QR Link and Open/Show QR quick actions.
- Full Analytics v2 dashboard with period filters, KPIs, trend, top QR, countries, devices, browsers, OS, platform, attribution confidence and per-QR drill-down.
- Derived analytics tables and bounded asynchronous aggregation.
- Same-origin `/backend` bridge.
- Installer patches the existing API startup and protects the authoritative production DB.

## FAIL -> correction during development

Early local implementation used an INSERT trigger to update analytics when a raw scan was inserted. That would put analytics work on the QR scan/redirect request path. It was rejected before packaging. Final design writes the raw scan normally and processes analytics in bounded background batches. Delete reconciliation only applies to scan IDs that have already been processed.
